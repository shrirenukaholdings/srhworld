Equities

Market-Linked

These are market-linked investments and don’t offer any assured returns. Our team of experts studies various stocks and industries to identify the most profitable opportunities.

With an in-depth technical and fundamental analysis, and risk-return ratio, the SRH team offers the best equity investments.

Explore Equity Strategies

Mutual Funds

Systematic Compounding

Indian mutual fund houses offer multiple types of schemes like equity, debt, and balanced funds. Within these categories, you can choose large, medium, and small cap funds, index funds, and many more options.

We provide clients with systematic investment plans, systematic transfer plans, and systematic withdrawal plans to meet their changing financial goals over the years.

Explore Mutual Funds

PMS (Portfolio Management)

Customised Portfolio

Selecting the right financial advisor is an important decision based on trust that your advisor has your best interests in mind at all times. The primary goal of our portfolio management services is to find solutions that are aligned to your personal goals and requirements.

Our entire team focuses on matching your risk appetite with the most efficient investments. SRH does not offer any “model portfolio” or a generic list of asset classes. Through on-going discussions and regular interactions, our team designs an investment portfolio that is strategically aligned to your risk-return goals while keeping in mind any investing constraints you may face.

SRH believes in creating and maintaining deep, meaningful, and personal relationships with all of its clients.

Explore PMS

Structured Product

Low-Risk High Returns

NCDs bear a fixed maturity date and the interest is paid either on maturity or at periodic intervals. This asset class when chosen correctly can deliver low-risk high returns along with tax benefits.

Our experts evaluate various NCDs on factors like credit rating, debt levels, interest coverage ratio, non-performing assets provisions, and capital adequacy ratio to maximize your returns.

Explore NCDs

Bonds

Fixed Income

Bonds are a popular fixed-income asset class offering assured albeit lower returns. Companies, banks, and even mutual funds offer different types of bonds. Further, you can invest in RBI savings bonds and sovereign gold bonds.

The SRH team analyzes the different bond offerings and evaluates the issuer’s performance and the ratings to recommend the best products for your portfolio.

Explore Bonds

Fixed Deposite

Optimal Tax Planning

SRH helps in optimal tax planning for its clients and offers corporate fixed deposits, commercial and industrial real estate rental property investments, and fixed income insurance plans.

Explore Fixed Deposits

Loans

Structured Credit

SRH has well-established and long-standing relationships with all the major banks and non-banking finance companies (NBFCs) across India. Micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs) and small and medium enterprises (SMEs) are the backbone for the future growth of the Indian economy. These businesses provide employment to millions of people and aid in industrialization of the rural and backward regions.

SRH works with MSMEs and SMEs to procure structured credit through its institutional relationships.
