Bonds

Fixed Income

Bonds are a reliable fixed-income asset class offering assured returns. We facilitate access to sovereign gold bonds, RBI savings bonds, public sector undertakings (PSU), and high-grade corporate bonds.

Our desk rigorously evaluates issuer credit ratings, debt coverage ratios, and cash-flow health to optimize steady, risk-mitigated yields.
