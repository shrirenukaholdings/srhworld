Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs)

NCDs bear a fixed maturity date and the interest is paid either on maturity or at periodic intervals. This asset class when chosen correctly can deliver low-risk high returns along with tax benefits.

Our experts evaluate various NCDs on factors like credit rating, debt levels, interest coverage ratio, non-performing assets provisions, and capital adequacy ratio to maximize your returns.

Unlisted Stocks & Block Deals

Unlisted stocks: Unlisted companies are privately held and have not yet gone through the IPO process and the primary benefit of investing in these stocks is that you get an early advantage of accessing new-age innovative businesses. With our wide network and extensive relationships with companies, we provide the best deals on unlisted stocks directly from the promoters via private placements or through intermediaries.

Block deals: These are trades where more than 500,000 shares or shares exceeding INR 5 crores in a listed company are executed. Such deals are conducted only during specific trading windows within a certain price range. SRH experts source the best block deals for its high-net worth clients via intermediaries or directly from large broking houses.

Market Offerings

IPO, OFS & New Fund Offerings (NFO)

Initial public offerings: IPOs are flooding the equity markets and investors are spoilt for choice and often identifying the best IPO is tricky. With a detailed study and analysis of the company, IPO end use of funds, industry outlook, order book, price band, and several other parameters, we recommend the best IPOs for our clients.

Offer for sale: Companies may require additional funds even after raising money via an IPO and opt for OFS. The promoters dilute their shareholding via an exchange platform and all categories of investors can bid for these shares. Based on the future growth prospects of the company and its reputation, we recommend the most profitable OFS investments for our clients.

New fund offerings: NFOs are first-time subscription offers in new mutual fund schemes and lasts for a few weeks. Our experts study the Scheme Information Document to understand the investment philosophy of the offer. Other factors like the AMC track record, performance of the fund manager, and scheme-related expenses are also analyzed before recommending.
