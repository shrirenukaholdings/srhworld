Derivative Analysis

Derivative products derive their value from an underlying asset like commodities, shares, or currencies. These are used for arbitrage, hedging, and speculation opportunities.

The two common types of derivative products are Futures and Options (F&O). A futures contract is an agreement wherein two parties agree to sell or buy an asset at a predetermined price on a fixed date. An options contract gives the parties a right without any obligation to buy or sell an asset at a particular price on a stated date.

Our analysts study and analyze various indicators, such as Put Call Ratio (PCR), rollover, open interest, and volatility (including implied and historic volatility) to recommend F&O investments that align with clients’ risk and returns goals.
