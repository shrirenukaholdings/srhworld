About Shri Renuka Holdings

Pioneering Disciplined Wealth Creation Since 2004

Founded in 2004, Shri Renuka Holdings (SRH) is a well-known wealth management company that has been delivering specialized services with over 18 years of experience in the equity markets and 15 years in futures and options (F&O).

SRH provides portfolio management services to help clients plan their financial future and achieve their goals faster with minimal risks.

Mission

We help clients build sustainable wealth over the long-term.

Vision

  • Ensure financial stability and security of our clients
  • Help clients achieve their financial goals
  • Create family wealth
  • Assist clients in building effective succession plans
